Our Departments

Expert care tailored to your specific needs, delivered by renowned specialists across a wide range of medical fields.

medical_services

General Medicine

Comprehensive care for adult illnesses, preventive medicine, and chronic disease management. Our general physicians are the first point of contact for patients seeking general medical care and non-surgical treatments.

local_hospital

General Surgery

Advanced surgical interventions ranging from routine appendectomies to complex abdominal procedures. Our team focuses heavily on minimally invasive techniques to ensure faster recovery times and minimal scarring.

pregnant_woman

Gynecology & Obstetrics

Complete women's healthcare, from maternity and high-risk pregnancies to advanced gynecological treatments. We provide a safe, nurturing environment for childbirth and specialized care for all stages of a woman's life.

accessible_forward

Orthopedics

Specialized care for musculoskeletal conditions, joint replacements, trauma care, and sports injuries. Our orthopedic surgeons utilize the latest technology to restore mobility and improve quality of life.

hearing

ENT (Ear, Nose, Throat)

Expert diagnosis and treatment for conditions affecting the ear, nose, throat, head, and neck. We offer comprehensive solutions for hearing loss, sinus issues, voice disorders, and pediatric ENT conditions.

ecg_heart

Cardiology

State-of-the-art cardiovascular care, heart disease prevention, and advanced cardiac diagnostics. Our cardiology department is equipped to handle emergencies and provide ongoing management of chronic heart conditions.

water_drop

Nephrology

Comprehensive kidney care, including dialysis services and management of chronic kidney diseases. We provide holistic care plans to preserve kidney function and improve patient outcomes.

science

Urology

Advanced treatments for urinary tract conditions and male reproductive health issues. Our urologists are skilled in both medical management and surgical interventions for conditions like kidney stones and prostate issues.

dentistry

Dentistry

Full-spectrum dental care including preventive, restorative, and advanced implant surgeries. We ensure a painless and comfortable experience for all our dental patients.

psychology

Neurology

Specialized care for neurological disorders, stroke management, and advanced brain and nerve diagnostics. Our neurologists are trained to handle complex cases with precision and compassion.

child_care

Pediatrics

Compassionate, expert healthcare for infants, children, and adolescents. Our pediatric wing is designed to be child-friendly, ensuring that our youngest patients feel safe and comfortable during their treatment.
