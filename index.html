About Apex Care Hospital & Research Centre

Pioneering Healthcare in Dewas

Apex Care Hospital & Research Centre is a 40-bedded premier medical institution in Dewas. Being a multi-speciality hospital, we provide services in over 28 medical specialties: Cardiology, Neurology, Orthopedics, Gastroenterology, Obstetrics and Gynaecology, Pediatrics, ENT, Oncology, and General Surgery, to name a few.

Milestones:

With our flagship being our dedicated Trauma Center and Intensive Care units,

  • Our Advanced Infrastructure features 3 Modern Operation Theaters, including Modular OT with Laminar Flow.
  • We offer the best ICU, NICU & PICU facilities in the region.
  • We provide 24/7 Emergency Support with a dedicated Trauma Center for Head Injuries.
  • We house Comprehensive Diagnostics (Pathology, X-Ray, Endoscopy, TMT, ECG, EEG).
